Chapter 2: Concepts and Definitions

A household is a group of persons who normally live together and take their meals from a common kitchen. Members of a household may or may not be related to one another by blood or marriage. The usual principal activity status of a person is determined on the basis of the activity on which the person spent a relatively long time during the 365 days preceding the date of survey.

A person is considered unemployed if the person did not work but was seeking or available for work during the reference period. The labour force participation rate is the percentage of persons in the labour force in the population. The worker population ratio is the percentage of employed persons in the population. The unemployment rate is the percentage of persons unemployed among the persons in the labour force.
